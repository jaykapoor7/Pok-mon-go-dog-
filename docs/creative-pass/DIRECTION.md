## Final direction

Product clarity first: immediately state what StrayPaw does, show the actual product relationship, and make the next action easy. Four sections and the locked marketing headlines remain. No photo-led direction, generated imagery, fantasy neighborhood or speculative dashboard redesign.

Palette: ink navy #0B1E3D, shell cream #F3EDE4, flame coral #F05B40, blue #2457CE. DM Sans carries product clarity; Instrument Serif carries editorial headings. Existing font definitions are preserved.

Hero: the app's real geographic outline with actual public city rollups, rendered on the server. Only cities with records and verified coordinates become markers. No fake map pins, animal identities or count placeholders. Three sourced hero metrics and their definitions remain unchanged. Unavailable data is stated honestly. The preview links to the working Living Map.

One Request: a local, clearly illustrative WhatsApp Send turns into a shared review folder. Assignment remains unassigned, care unrecorded, follow-up unscheduled and animal record unlinked. The receipt says no organisation has accepted the example. On mobile the record replaces the message in the same space, with immediate Review queue / Animal history views; Replay restores the message. Desktop shows the report moving along the connection. The map is a bounded sample of real coarse public area centers, and explicitly does not include the example.

One Ecosystem: immediate keyboard/touch audience switching, existing real software previews, and a coarse public geographic preview. No ornamental audience cards or camera motion.

The Company: a compact editorial close with a small, credited human-made Rajasthani dog painting. Folk-art details remain subtle, sourced background accents. No AI-generated images or stock photographs are included.

Navigation: floating cream capsule, navy frame and tactile offset, stable height and functional mobile navigation with contained/restored focus. Buttons: rounded, ink-edged, coral/cream fills and quick tactile hover/press feedback, as explicitly requested by the user.

Motion: one 600ms map preview arrival, one optional report transfer and short folder feedback; 140–200ms control feedback. No pinning, scroll hijacking, reveal-hidden reading text, loops or added animation library. Reduced motion presents the complete still state immediately. Core information and links survive without JavaScript.

## Skill direction and exceptions

Taste draw: **Loud and flat**, seed **1007166845**. Used subject inventory, flat fields, scale and distinct compositions. User copy and latest requests override editable headlines, accordions, pinned portraits, photo minimum, square buttons and broad automatic motion requirements. The generic house-style score is not a release gate; actual accessibility, responsive behavior, factual data and the brief are.

## Scope

Marketing only. Existing server reads remain bounded and parallel. No migration, security/RLS change, dependency addition, dashboard redesign or operational workflow change. Product, Solutions, Our Story and Contact keep their approved copy and functional behavior; their CTA shape follows the restored button vocabulary.
