# One Health demo: Loom script (about 4 minutes 45 seconds)

**Open:** https://demo.straypaw.org (it opens the One Health demo). Use Chrome, a 1920×1080 window, 100% zoom.
**Before you press record:** open the page fresh; **Start Demo** always begins a clean run (see "Before recording"
at the end). Every person, patient and record is fictional.

The guide is the small card in a corner. It always says what to click, type or say, rings the real control on the
page, ticks the action when it sees it done, and moves on by itself. **Next action** skips ahead; when a screen is
not open yet the main button reads **Take me there**. At the end of a stage it reads **Next role**. **Back**,
**Replay stage**, **Restart** and **Exit** are always on the card; Exit folds it to a pill you can resume from.

Status marks on the card, in the grant's terms: **Live and tested** · **In staging** (built and tested on the
demonstration database, not yet in programme use) · **Planned for the pilot** · **Awaiting integration**.

## 1 · Citizen reporting and AI intake (0:20 to 1:05)

**On screen:** Kaju's bite help: the **Chat** with Kaju on the left, **Kaju's report** on the right (empty), and a
**Call** tab. Kaju has already given first aid and asked what happened.

| # | Click, type or say | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | In the chat, type (or press **Example**): *"A person was bitten by a stray dog near Indiranagar this morning. They need medical assistance."* and press send. | Kaju types back what it understood: a bite, by a street dog, in Indiranagar, this morning, looking for medical help. **Kaju's report** fills in: what happened, animal, area, when, who was hurt. |
| 2 | **No, none of these** (the fixed danger-sign question) | "Please see a doctor today…", then Kaju offers to tell the nearest anti-rabies centre. |
| 3 | **Yes, tell the clinic** | Kaju asks only what was missing: what the dog looked like. |
| 4 | Type *"Brown dog, no collar, usually near the bus stop on 100 Feet Road"* (or **Example**), send, then **No, don't call me** | **Your report** in the chat; the report on the right reads "Ready: waiting for your OK to send", with the landmark picked out. |
| 5 | **Send the report** | "Sent to Teaching Hospital Anti-Rabies Clinic. They know you're coming." Then **Where to go today**, with that clinic first, marked **Expecting you**. |

**To show the voice call instead** (optional, adds about 30 seconds): press **Call**, then **Call Kaju**, allow the
microphone, and answer aloud: the opening words above, then "No", "Yes", the description, "No", "Send it". Kaju
speaks every question, the caption shows what it heard, the report fills in the same way, and the call ends with
"Report sent". Every answer can also be tapped on the call screen.

> "A resident who's been bitten opens Kaju. First aid comes first, and it's fixed wording. Then she just says what
> happened. Kaju pulls out the place, the time and the animal, and you can watch the report build on the right.
> One safety question, the advice to see a doctor today, and then Kaju offers to tell the clinic she's coming,
> asking only for what it still needs. Nothing is sent until she confirms, and then she's told exactly where to go.
> It's the same conversation by phone: Kaju asks aloud and listens. Today the call runs in the browser; a public
> helpline number, and calls in Kannada, are part of the pilot. No AI model reads her words here: Kaju uses fixed
> rules on her own device. WhatsApp reports land in the same place, and that live connection is waiting on Meta's
> verification."

**Marks:** Kaju chat, the browser voice call and the report *In staging* · the public helpline number and Kannada calls *Planned for the pilot* · WhatsApp Business *Awaiting integration*.

**Then click:** **Next role: Hospital**.

## 2 · Hospital and human-health response (1:05 to 1:40)

| # | Click, type or say | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Under **Notices from the public**, the Indiranagar notice: **Patient came in** | **Record a bite** opens, already filled from her notice. |
| 2 | Guide: **Fill the example entry**, then **Save presentation** and **Save** | The bite record: Category III, recorded in the clinician's name. |
| 3 | Guide: **Fill the example prescription**, then **Save prescription**, tick "This is the course I prescribed", **Save prescription** | Dose 1 today, dose 2, 3 and 4 on the dates entered. |
| 4 | Look at **Animal follow-up** (right) | "Waiting for the team to report what they found" · Animal Care Trust. |
| 5 | **Next action** (optional) | **Facilities**: vaccine, immunoglobulin and wound washing as each facility last confirmed them, with dates. |

> "The clinic already has her notice before she walks in. The clinician checks it with her, records Category III and
> prescribes the course. StrayPaw records the clinician's decisions. It never assesses the wound or suggests a
> schedule, and every dose date is the clinician's. Each dose then shows up on the clinic's Today. Recording the bite
> also sent a follow-up request to the NGO that covers Indiranagar, with the place and the dog's description and
> nothing about the patient. Facilities confirm vaccine, immunoglobulin and wound washing, and referrals send people
> where a service is missing."

**Marks:** bite register, PEP courses, follow-up requests, service confirmations *In staging* · SMS or WhatsApp reminders *Awaiting integration* (today they are logged phone calls) · real facility data from the health department *Awaiting integration*.

## 4 · Kaju coordinates the response (2:30 to 3:30)

| # | Click, type or say | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask: *"What's happening with the Indiranagar bite incident? What's missing, and what do the teams need to do next?"* (or **Use the example question**), press **Enter** | Kaju answers: the request, who sent it, its status and due date; Simba and its history; **Missing**: the ten-day observation is not planned, and the rabies vaccination is 391 days old, past the programme's 365-day interval; **Who does what** for the hospital, the NGO and the programme. Links under it open the request, Simba and Simba's case. |
| 2 | Point at the links and **What Kaju checked** | Each fact opens its record. |
| 3 | **Confirm** on **Open a case** and on **Schedule a follow-up** | Each shows *Saved*, in your name, with Kaju's help noted. |
| 4 | **Next action** (optional) | **Kaju noticed**, missions, and **What Kaju can use**, where email, SMS and WhatsApp say plainly they are not connected. |

> "This is where Kaju earns its place. I ask in plain words what's going on. Kaju reads only what this coordinator is
> allowed to see. That means the hospital's request, the dog and its records, and never the patient or their doses.
> It tells me what's missing: nobody has planned the ten-day observation, and Simba's rabies vaccination has lapsed.
> It says who's responsible for what, and every fact links back to its record. Then it prepares the work, an
> observation case and a revaccination follow-up, and waits for me to confirm. Nothing is saved until a person says
> so. On this demo Kaju runs without a paid AI model: these answers come from its fixed rules over real records, and
> it says so under the answer. Free-form answers with a model are part of the pilot."

**Marks:** Kaju's record-linked answers, prepared work and approvals *Live and tested* · the cross-partner incident briefing *In staging* · free-form answers with a connected model *Planned for the pilot* · email, SMS, WhatsApp sending *Awaiting integration*.

## If something does not work while recording

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| The call says the microphone is blocked, or the browser cannot listen | Press **Continue in the chat** (or the **Chat** tab) and use **Example**. The call needs Chrome, Edge or Safari and the microphone allowed for demo.straypaw.org (Brave switches the speech service off). |
| A question is not answered and the guide waits | Press **Next action**: on the citizen's questions it answers for you and moves on. |
| Kaju's answer shows an error | Press **Try again** under it. Kaju needs no outside AI service here, so only a lost connection stops it. |
| The highlighted control is not where you are | Press **Take me there** on the guide. |
| A screen is still loading ("Waiting for the screen to show it…") | Wait two seconds; the demo database is in Mumbai. |
| A step went wrong | **Replay stage** starts the stage again; **Restart** starts a fresh run from the citizen. |
| You want to show something else | **Exit** folds the guide; the pill resumes exactly where you were. |

## Before recording

1. In a fresh Chrome window open https://demo.straypaw.org and press **Start Demo** when you start recording.
   Each run creates its own fictional notice, bite, request and Kaju case, so earlier rehearsals never get in the way.
2. To clear every rehearsal from the demonstration database (optional, owner only): run
   `supabase/seed/one_health_demo.sql` in the staging SQL editor. It removes and recreates only the One Health demo
   rows. See `docs/one-health/README.md` §5.
3. Allow the microphone when Chrome asks, if you will show the call.
