# StrayPaw 5: product design pass (9 November 2026)

Branch `claude/straypaw-product-redesign-eyszyc`, based on `main` at `6bb02c1`.
Nothing was deployed, no migration was run, no database was read or written.

## How it was inspected

The app was run locally and looked at in Chromium at 1440, 834 and 390 px.

- Public pages: rendered as a signed-out visitor.
- Signed-in workspaces: rendered in the repository's own illustrative mode
  (`NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO_RECORDING=true`, "Coastal Paws Trust" and the "Zone 13"
  reviewing office), with no Supabase URL configured, so no request could
  reach a real project.
- The older local QA harness (`e2e/harness`) had fallen behind the app: it did
  not answer the membership lookups the shell makes, so every signed-in page
  stayed on its loading skeleton. It now answers them.

## What changed

### Application

- **One frame** (`src/sp/shell/app4.css`, section "StrayPaw 5"). Every page
  title now starts 32 px from the sidebar (16 px on a phone), on the same line
  as its tabs and table. Re-measured on the same routes: all 32.
- **One name per place** (`workspaces.ts`, `Shell.tsx`, `Work.tsx`,
  `Quality.tsx`). Sidebar, breadcrumb, title and phone tab agree.
- **Sidebar**: one line-icon family, quiet section labels, clearer selected
  row, a smaller Kaju corner.
- **Top bar**: Back only on a record or form; section tabs do not show on a
  record.
- **Ground**: plain white. The drifting glow and pointer light are removed.
- **Today**: the five stages are one connected strip on a desk and a list on
  a phone and tablet. "not read" is said at text size.
- **Work queue**: the empty-state import prompt is one row; the grid bug is
  fixed.
- **Kaju panel** no longer covers the top bar.
- **Government overview** has a title.

### Public site

- **Homepage** (`src/app/page.tsx`, `src/sp5/home/`). Order: hero (what
  StrayPaw is, on the existing live product window) → partners → the problem
  (five places → one record) → five steps (existing pinned scroller, retitled)
  → the package from both sides → audit trail → Kaju → integrations with their
  real status → recorded results → a three-step pilot → questions and close.
- The pop-over is removed from the homepage.
- Kaju's "teammate", "recurring work" and "inside each tool" sections moved to
  `/kaju-agents`; nothing was deleted.
- Every record shown is illustrative and labelled. No new figures, customers
  or capabilities are claimed. Pilot copy restates the existing FAQ answers.

## Navigation architecture (unchanged in structure, unified in naming)

The registry in `src/sp/shell/workspaces.ts` stays the single source. Routes,
role rules, flags and deep links are untouched.

**NGO**
- Overview: Today
- Operations: Work queue (tabs: Work queue, Field mode, New case) · Intake ·
  Treatment & sign-off · Release evidence
- Records: Animals (tabs: Animals, Map, Care ledger, Identity review, Data
  quality) · Programmes (tabs: Programmes, Working zones, Surveys & census,
  Feeding spots, Projects)
- Reporting: Reports (tabs: Report packages, Contracts & reconciliation,
  Reports & proof, Evidence files)
- Team: Team, Settings, Connections, Workflow policies (by role)
- Kaju: Ask Kaju
- Phone: Today · Queue · Intake · Animals · Kaju; vets and field staff keep
  their own four.

**Government**
- Overview: Overview · Wards & map
- Decisions: Review submissions · Contracts & rates
- Reporting: Programmes · Reports
- Organisation: Connections
- Kaju: Ask Kaju

## Tradeoffs

- **Layering.** The styles are still a stack of override layers
  (`sp.css` → `shell3` → `app3` → `app4`). This pass adds one clearly marked
  section at the end of `app4.css` rather than a new file. Collapsing the
  stack into one token file is the right next step and is a larger, riskier
  change than this pass should carry.
- **Glow removed.** DESIGN.md 4.0 specified a faint glow. It added motion and
  gradient behind working data without explaining any state; section 0 of
  DESIGN.md now records the change.
- **Homepage leads with the platform, not Kaju.** Kaju keeps its own section,
  its announcement bar and its page.

## Not done in this pass

- Animal and case detail pages could not be rendered with data locally (they
  read tables the illustrative mode does not answer), so they were not
  redesigned. They inherit the frame, top bar and sidebar changes.
- The Community and Education workspaces were not reviewed.
- Secondary public pages (`/platform`, `/for-ngos`, `/for-governments`,
  `/pricing`) were checked for regressions only.
- AnimalID and data-infrastructure branches were not touched.
