## Direction and scope

On this page, StrayPaw's shared field record becomes a playable record desk. The interaction is the memorable element: one visitor action becomes a visible review item, with ownership deliberately left pending until the visitor tries the example decision. Unknown animal identity, care and follow-up stay unknown.

Use the existing navy/cream/coral/blue, DM Sans and Instrument Serif, plus an earthy green section ground. Keep the floating navigation and round tactile buttons. No scroll choreography. The hero uses an existing licensed Coromandel palampore, not newly commissioned neighborhood art. New commissioned neighborhood artwork remains an art opportunity; no generated image is substituted for it.
